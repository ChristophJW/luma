Luma · Visual identity

A private camera for the day itself. Guests capture a handful of frames; the whole album stays dark until the event is over — then it's revealed together. The mark is that held light.

The mark

One glint of light

Luma is luminance — the brightness a sensor actually records. The mark is a single glint of it: a four-point sparkle with concave edges, drawn full enough to survive at favicon size and sharp enough to read as light, not a star or a flower.

It stands in for the brand wherever the wordmark won't fit — app icon, avatar, the shutter you tap to capture.

Lockup & color

Four grounds, one accent

The wordmark is set in Fraunces, the house display face. Amber is the only accent it ever takes; the letters are ink or paper, never colored. Pick the pairing that fits the surface.

Palette

Warm neutrals, one light

Paper and ink both carry a trace of amber, so nothing on screen reads clinical. Glow is the accent — the light itself. Safelight, the darkroom red, is held back for a single moment: the beat before an album is revealed.
